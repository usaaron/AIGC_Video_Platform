knowledge_id: knowledge.character.action_learning_cycle.v1
version: v1
category: character_design
principle: Character progression can be expressed as a cycle of pursuing a desire, learning from consequences, deciding, and changing the next action.
rationale: The cycle links revelation to agency and prevents information scenes from becoming passive exposition.
when_to_use:
  - Connecting scene outcomes across an episode.
  - Designing a reveal that should alter behavior.
when_not_to_use:
  - To require every new fact to cause an immediate rational adjustment.
  - When denial or repetition is a deliberate character flaw with visible cost.
application_rules:
  - After a meaningful discovery, specify the resulting decision.
  - Make the next tactic different because of what was learned.
anti_patterns:
  - The protagonist learns decisive information but follows the same plan unchanged.
  - A decision appears without any preceding pressure or evidence.
examples:
  - weak: She learns her ally lied and continues sharing every secret.
  - stronger: She feeds the ally controlled information to test who receives it.
source_reference:
  source_title: The Anatomy of Story

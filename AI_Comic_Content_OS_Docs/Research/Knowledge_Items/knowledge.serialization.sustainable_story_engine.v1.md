knowledge_id: knowledge.serialization.sustainable_story_engine.v1
version: v1
category: story_structure_and_serialization
principle: A continuing series should demonstrate a sustainable dramatic engine that can generate related but non-repetitive goals, opposition, choices, and consequences over time.
rationale: A single unresolved problem can describe an ending but does not by itself prove that the middle can sustain many distinct story movements.
when_to_use:
  - Assessing whether a Story Bible can support a long serialized work.
  - Distinguishing ongoing story potential from a stretched single plot.
when_not_to_use:
  - To force an episodic reset pattern onto a finite serial.
  - To justify extending a story after its dramatic engine is exhausted.
application_rules:
  - Name the repeatable source of dramatic action and how its consequences accumulate rather than reset.
  - State how protagonist action and opposition response can evolve across multiple movements.
  - Identify an exhaustion condition that signals the story should end, transform, or gain new material.
anti_patterns:
  - The protagonist repeats the same investigation action while only the clue names change.
  - Target episode count is presented as proof that the concept is sustainable.
examples:
  - weak: Each arc finds another witness who is immediately silenced.
  - stronger: Each evidence cycle changes the protagonist's hypothesis, public position, alliances, and the opponent's strategy.

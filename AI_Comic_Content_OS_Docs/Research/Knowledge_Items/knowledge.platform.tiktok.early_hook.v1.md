knowledge_id: knowledge.platform.tiktok.early_hook.v1
version: v1
category: short_form_tiktok
principle: For TikTok-oriented short-form experiments, communicate the central value, tension, surprise, or emotional question early enough that viewers can understand why to continue.
rationale: TikTok's advertising research emphasizes that a large share of recall impact occurs in the opening seconds and recommends early hooks using suspense, surprise, or emotion.
when_to_use:
  - Testing opening beats for TikTok-oriented vertical scripts.
  - Comparing whether the first scene establishes a concrete viewing motive.
when_not_to_use:
  - As a universal six-second law for organic scripted drama.
  - To replace setup with disconnected shock or exposition.
application_rules:
  - Make the opening hook legible through action, image, dialogue, or a precise dramatic question.
  - Connect the hook to the episode's actual conflict and payoff.
anti_patterns:
  - A sensational opening image never affects the story.
  - The first scene spends its full duration explaining background before conflict appears.
examples:
  - weak: A generic threat is shown, followed by unrelated setup.
  - stronger: The protagonist's first action creates a visible risk that the episode must resolve or intensify.

knowledge_id: knowledge.visual.channel_separation.v1
version: v1
category: visual_narrative
principle: Keep visual action, spoken dialogue, voiceover, internal monologue, and on-screen text semantically distinguishable so each channel has a clear production and storytelling function.
rationale: A practitioner-oriented dynamic-comic format separates these channels to make scripts easier to read and translate into production, even though its exact symbols are not an industry standard.
when_to_use:
  - Reviewing whether a script communicates what is seen, heard, thought, or displayed.
  - Preparing future format adapters without changing MasterScript semantics.
when_not_to_use:
  - To adopt the source's triangle symbols or Chinese production syntax as mandatory.
  - To assume every project needs all channels.
application_rules:
  - Label the semantic role of non-dialogue text.
  - Use voiceover and internal monologue only when their information cannot be delivered more effectively through action.
anti_patterns:
  - Internal thought is mixed into action as if visible.
  - Voiceover, dialogue, and captions repeat the same sentence.
examples:
  - weak: Action prose ambiguously includes a private thought.
  - stronger: The visible hesitation, spoken denial, and internal fear are represented as distinct signals.

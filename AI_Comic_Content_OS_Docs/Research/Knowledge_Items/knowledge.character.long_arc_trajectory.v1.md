knowledge_id: knowledge.character.long_arc_trajectory.v1
version: v1
category: character_design
principle: A major serialized character should have an ongoing struggle, contradiction, and trajectory that explains how repeated pressure can produce development without arbitrary personality changes.
rationale: Biography describes who a character was; a longform trajectory explains what will repeatedly test them and what kinds of change or resistance remain possible.
when_to_use:
  - Writing major-character sections of a Story Bible.
  - Checking continuity across macro movements.
when_not_to_use:
  - To require every character to transform positively.
  - To predetermine every choice so tightly that scenes lose discovery and surprise.
application_rules:
  - Define the persistent struggle and contradiction that can be tested more than once.
  - Identify a direction of change, resistance, deterioration, or reset appropriate to the format.
  - Connect trajectory milestones to choices and consequences, not only revelations about backstory.
anti_patterns:
  - A character changes values only because the plot requires a twist.
  - The same emotional lesson is learned and forgotten in every arc.
examples:
  - weak: She slowly learns to trust over hundreds of episodes.
  - stronger: Each alliance tests whether she can share control without surrendering her evidence or moral boundary, with trust producing increasingly irreversible costs.

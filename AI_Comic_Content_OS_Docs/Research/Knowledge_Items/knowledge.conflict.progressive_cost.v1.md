knowledge_id: knowledge.conflict.progressive_cost.v1
version: v1
category: conflict_and_emotion
principle: Escalate conflict by increasing the cost, narrowing the options, or deepening the opposition created by earlier actions.
rationale: Progressive pressure prevents scenes from repeating equivalent versions of the same obstacle.
when_to_use:
  - Sequencing multiple conflicts across an episode.
  - Evaluating whether pressure genuinely rises.
when_not_to_use:
  - To make every beat louder or more violent.
  - When a temporary release is needed to make the next escalation legible.
application_rules:
  - Track what becomes harder, riskier, or less reversible after each outcome.
  - Vary escalation through relational, informational, moral, temporal, or physical cost.
anti_patterns:
  - Three scenes repeat the same threat with different wording.
  - Stakes are announced as larger but available choices remain unchanged.
examples:
  - weak: The antagonist warns her three times.
  - stronger: The warning becomes exposure, which forces her to choose between evidence and an innocent ally.

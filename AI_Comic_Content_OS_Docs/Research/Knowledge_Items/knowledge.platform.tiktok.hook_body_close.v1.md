category: short_form_tiktok
principle: "Use hook, development, and close as a lightweight communication check, not as a rigid plot formula: attract attention, deliver the promised dramatic movement, then anchor the ending."
rationale: TikTok's Creative Codes recommend a hook-body-close structure for ads; the transferable hypothesis is that short-form units benefit from a clear opening promise, meaningful middle, and purposeful terminal beat.
when_to_use:
  - Reviewing short episode completeness.
  - Detecting openings that promise one story while the body delivers another.
when_not_to_use:
  - To force a commercial call-to-action into fiction.
  - To override genre-specific pacing or serialized open endings.
application_rules:
  - Define the hook's promise, the body's development, and the close's dramatic function.
  - For fiction, translate the close into payoff, reversal, or cliffhanger rather than a sales CTA.
anti_patterns:
  - The body does not develop the opening question.
  - The final cliffhanger is attached without setup.
examples:
  - weak: A betrayal hook leads to unrelated romance scenes and a random monster reveal.
  - stronger: The romance scenes expose why the betrayal matters, and the close reframes who benefited from it.

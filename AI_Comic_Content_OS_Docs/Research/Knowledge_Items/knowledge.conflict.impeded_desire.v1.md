---
knowledge_id: knowledge.conflict.impeded_desire.v1
version: v1
category: conflict_and_emotion
principle: Build conflict by placing meaningful resistance between a character and a specific desire.
rationale: Obstruction turns intention into struggle and exposes tactics, priorities, and vulnerability.
when_to_use:
  - Defining a scene conflict from its goal.
  - Checking whether conflict affects the protagonist directly.
when_not_to_use:
  - To require physical confrontation.
  - When the dramatic tension comes from incompatible internal commitments rather than an external opponent.
application_rules:
  - Name what the character wants now and what actively blocks it.
  - Make resistance force adaptation, sacrifice, or disclosure.
anti_patterns:
  - General danger exists but does not obstruct the scene goal.
  - Characters argue about facts neither of them needs to act on.
examples:
  - weak: A storm creates atmosphere during a negotiation.
  - stronger: The storm cuts communication, forcing the negotiator to trust the person she planned to expose.

knowledge_id: knowledge.character.change_under_struggle.v1
version: v1
category: character_design
principle: Character change should emerge from sustained struggle against resistance, or the story should make the cost of refusing change visible.
rationale: Transformation feels earned when pressure tests existing habits, beliefs, and tactics.
when_to_use:
  - Designing emotional movement across scenes or episodes.
  - Distinguishing an arc from a last-minute declaration.
when_not_to_use:
  - To force every character into positive growth.
  - In static-character forms where the protagonist changes the surrounding world instead.
application_rules:
  - Identify the old weakness or flawed pattern, the pressure that exposes its limit, and the consequential response.
  - If the character does not change, dramatize what that refusal costs.
anti_patterns:
  - A final speech announces growth unsupported by previous decisions.
  - Trauma alone is treated as character development.
examples:
  - weak: He says he trusts others after acting alone throughout.
  - stronger: He gives an ally control of the escape route when secrecy would be safer for him personally.

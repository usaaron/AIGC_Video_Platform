category: short_form_tiktok
principle: Treat platform storytelling frameworks as adaptable starting patterns whose usefulness depends on story, audience, and purpose, not as universal templates.
rationale: TikTok's own framework guidance says the formats must be adapted to the specific business; fictional narrative requires an additional transfer-and-validation step.
when_to_use:
  - Selecting a bounded opening or presentation experiment.
  - Comparing result-first, question-first, or process-first variants.
when_not_to_use:
  - To hard-code trends or advertising structures into the story ontology.
  - To assume a framework transfers from ads to drama without testing.
application_rules:
  - Name the framework, intended effect, and story-specific adaptation.
  - Validate the adapted form on fixed fixtures before promoting it to generation guidance.
anti_patterns:
  - Copying a current trend into every genre.
  - Treating a platform recommendation as proof of dramatic quality.
examples:
  - weak: Every episode opens at the climax regardless of mystery or payoff needs.
  - stronger: A result-first opening is tested only where it creates a causal question without spoiling the core reveal.

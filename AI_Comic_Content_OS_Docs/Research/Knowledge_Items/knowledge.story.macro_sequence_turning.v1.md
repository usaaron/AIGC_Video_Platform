---
knowledge_id: knowledge.story.macro_sequence_turning.v1
version: v1
category: story_structure
principle: Organize a long-arc story into content-derived sequences whose major escalations, revelations, or reversals change the direction or power balance of the narrative.
rationale: Macro sequences provide causal structure without requiring every branch to use the same act count, length, or depth.
when_to_use:
  - Decomposing an approved Story Bible into major story movements.
  - Reviewing whether adjacent planning nodes have distinct dramatic functions.
when_not_to_use:
  - To impose a fixed number of acts or equal-sized branches.
  - To label ordinary activity as a turning point without a state change.
application_rules:
  - Give each sequence a dramatic question, strategy, opposition, and irreversible exit state.
  - Mark whether its major turn is an escalation, revelation, reversal, or a justified combination.
  - Pass a concrete unresolved obligation into the next sequence.
anti_patterns:
  - Nodes differ only by episode range while repeating the same strategy and stakes.
  - A reveal changes exposition but not character choices or power.
examples:
  - weak: Phase two contains more investigation and greater danger.
  - stronger: Proof of institutional collusion changes the goal from identifying a killer to surviving a public counterattack without sacrificing the source.

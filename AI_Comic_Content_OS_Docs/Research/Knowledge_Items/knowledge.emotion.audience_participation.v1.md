knowledge_id: knowledge.emotion.audience_participation.v1
version: v1
category: conflict_and_emotion
principle: Emotional engagement strengthens when the audience can infer motives, anticipate consequences, and revise its understanding instead of receiving every meaning as explanation.
rationale: Participation joins feeling with interpretation and gives reveals emotional as well as informational force.
when_to_use:
  - Designing subtext, suspense, and relationship tension.
  - Deciding which emotional facts should be shown before being named.
when_not_to_use:
  - When essential context would otherwise be inaccessible or misleading.
  - To make character motivation so opaque that choices seem random.
application_rules:
  - Provide observable behavior that supports more than one live interpretation.
  - Resolve or complicate the question through later action.
anti_patterns:
  - Dialogue explains the exact emotion already visible in action.
  - Ambiguity has no eventual consequence or clarification.
examples:
  - weak: He says, "I am afraid to trust you."
  - stronger: He unlocks the door for her but keeps the evidence drive in his pocket.

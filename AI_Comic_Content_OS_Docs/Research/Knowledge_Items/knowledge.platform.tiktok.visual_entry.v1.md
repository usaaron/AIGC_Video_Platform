version: v1
category: short_form_tiktok
principle: Let the opening frame or action carry immediate visual information or suspense instead of relying only on delayed verbal explanation.
rationale: TikTok's storytelling-format guidance treats the opening as the place to create interest or suspense through a hook, question, or visual cue.
when_to_use:
  - Writing visually led openings for vertical short-form content.
  - Reviewing whether the first beat is readable without a long narration block.
when_not_to_use:
  - To ban dialogue-led openings with strong dramatic action.
  - To require spectacle when a precise expression, object, or contradiction is sufficient.
application_rules:
  - Identify the first visible change, contradiction, or unresolved situation.
  - Ensure that the image remains relevant to the episode's central action.
anti_patterns:
  - A decorative establishing shot delays the dramatic event.
  - Visual shock substitutes for understandable stakes.
examples:
  - weak: A city montage plays while narration explains the premise.
  - stronger: The engineer watches her own access log change in real time before she speaks.

knowledge_id: knowledge.story.transformation_state_contrast.v1
version: v1
category: story_structure
principle: Contrast the protagonist's and story world's observable opening state with the final state to test whether the narrative has produced meaningful transformation.
rationale: A clear state contrast makes change visible and helps distinguish an earned ending from a conclusion that merely stops the plot.
when_to_use:
  - Reviewing Story Bible ending direction and long character trajectory.
  - Checking whether a final macro movement pays off the initial dramatic condition.
when_not_to_use:
  - To require a positive or complete transformation.
  - To reduce complex change to a cosmetic visual difference.
application_rules:
  - Describe both states through observable behavior, relationships, power, belief, or environment.
  - Connect the final contrast to accumulated choices and consequences.
  - Allow tragic, resistant, cyclical, or incomplete change when it matches the story promise.
anti_patterns:
  - The final image changes location but the protagonist's choices and relationships remain unchanged.
  - Transformation is declared in narration without evidence in action.
examples:
  - weak: She begins alone and ends at a public ceremony, but still delegates every consequential choice.
  - stronger: She begins hiding evidence to survive and ends publicly accepting the cost of releasing it under her own name.

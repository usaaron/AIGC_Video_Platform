knowledge_id: knowledge.longform.structure_from_material.v1
version: v1
category: story_structure_and_serialization
principle: Longform structure should be shaped by the story's characters, environment, conflicts, and developing material rather than imposed as a rigid, equal-depth blueprint.
rationale: A long work needs deliberate organization, but its branches differ in dramatic weight and should not be expanded merely to satisfy symmetry or length.
when_to_use:
  - Choosing whether a StoryPlanNode should split again or become episode-ready.
  - Reviewing variable-depth recursive planning.
when_not_to_use:
  - To avoid planning or leave causality unspecified.
  - To claim that any improvised expansion is organically justified.
application_rules:
  - Base decomposition on changes in character struggle, conflict, event, and narrative purpose.
  - Permit different branches to use different depths and lengths.
  - Require a content reason for every additional node.
anti_patterns:
  - Every branch is forced into the same number of children.
  - A thin premise is expanded because the product target requests more episodes.
examples:
  - weak: Divide every phase into four equal arcs regardless of content.
  - stronger: Split the institutional conspiracy more deeply than the homecoming setup because it contains more independent actors, strategies, and irreversible turns.

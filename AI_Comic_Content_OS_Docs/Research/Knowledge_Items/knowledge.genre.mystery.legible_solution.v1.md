knowledge_id: knowledge.genre.mystery.legible_solution.v1
version: v1
category: genre_blueprint
principle: A mystery should sustain a puzzling question while making its eventual solution retrospectively legible through evidence available in the story.
genre_blueprint:
  audience_expectation: A meaningful question can be investigated and its solution can be understood from story evidence.
  emotional_promise: Curiosity, uncertainty, interpretive participation, and the satisfaction or disturbance of coherent discovery.
  common_story_engine: Evidence changes hypotheses; investigation encounters concealment; inference narrows possible explanations.
  common_failure_modes: Decisive facts are withheld, the solution is arbitrary, or investigation does not change the protagonist's choices.
  typical_conflict_patterns: Investigator versus concealment; appearance versus evidence; competing explanations; truth versus personal cost.
rationale: The audience's confidence that a solution exists supports active inference; a solution based on unavailable facts breaks that contract.
when_to_use:
  - Detective, investigation, hidden-truth, and puzzle-centered stories.
  - Designing reveal timing and clue placement.
when_not_to_use:
  - To require the audience to solve every mystery before the protagonist.
  - For deliberately unresolved existential mysteries whose genre promise is different.
application_rules:
  - Plant the material evidence used by the eventual inference.
  - Distinguish concealment of significance from concealment of the fact itself.
  - Let solving the question require interpretation, not merely a late confession.
anti_patterns:
  - The decisive clue appears only during the explanation.
  - The solution is selected from many equally unsupported possibilities.
examples:
  - weak: The AI confesses to a hidden capability never shown before.
  - stronger: Repeated timing anomalies become evidence that the AI has been editing the engineer's audit trail.
source_reference:

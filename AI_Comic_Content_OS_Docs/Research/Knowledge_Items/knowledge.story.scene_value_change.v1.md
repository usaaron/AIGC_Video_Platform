knowledge_id: knowledge.story.scene_value_change.v1
version: v1
category: story_structure
principle: A scene should turn at least one meaningful story value or state through conflict; activity without change is not sufficient dramatic movement.
rationale: State change gives a scene purpose and supplies consequences for the next scene.
when_to_use:
  - Reviewing whether Scene Outcome is materially different from Scene Goal.
  - Detecting filler in an episode draft.
when_not_to_use:
  - To require every scene to reverse the entire story.
  - To reject quiet scenes where relationship knowledge, trust, or emotional position changes.
application_rules:
  - Name the relevant before-state and after-state.
  - Tie the change to a conflict, choice, discovery, or cost inside the scene.
anti_patterns:
  - Characters discuss a problem and leave with the same knowledge and options.
  - Visual spectacle occurs without altering goals, stakes, or relationships.
examples:
  - weak: Two rivals argue and repeat their positions.
  - stronger: The argument forces one rival to reveal leverage that changes the other's plan.

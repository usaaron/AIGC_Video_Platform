knowledge_id: knowledge.character.choice_reveals_character.v1
version: v1
category: character_design
principle: Reveal character through consequential choices under pressure, especially when attractive values or outcomes conflict.
rationale: Traits stated in description are weak evidence; costly choice demonstrates the character's operative priorities.
when_to_use:
  - Evaluating character agency and moral boundaries.
  - Differentiating characters who face the same event.
when_not_to_use:
  - To make every choice extreme or irreversible.
  - When a low-pressure choice is intentionally establishing routine before disruption.
application_rules:
  - Present at least two meaningful options with distinct costs.
  - Let the chosen action affect later trust, risk, or opportunity.
anti_patterns:
  - The script labels a character brave without showing a risky choice.
  - A choice has only one plausible option and no cost.
examples:
  - weak: The narration calls her loyal.
  - stronger: She gives up her escape window to keep a promise to a witness.

knowledge_id: knowledge.visual.word_image_complement.v1
version: v1
category: visual_narrative
principle: Let words and images carry complementary narrative work instead of routinely duplicating the same information.
rationale: Deliberate division of labor increases information density while preserving the distinct strengths of visual and verbal channels.
when_to_use:
  - Reviewing dialogue, captions, internal monologue, and visual action together.
  - Designing visually readable emotional beats.
when_not_to_use:
  - When repetition is necessary for accessibility, emphasis, or deliberate irony.
  - To remove captions that provide essential nonvisual context.
application_rules:
  - State what the image communicates and what the words add or complicate.
  - Prefer contrast, subtext, context, or temporal information over literal duplication.
anti_patterns:
  - Narration describes exactly what the panel already shows.
  - Dialogue carries every action because the visual description is empty.
examples:
  - weak: Caption says she deletes the file while the image shows her deleting it.
  - stronger: The image shows deletion while the message she leaves behind reveals whom she is protecting.

---
knowledge_id: knowledge.conflict.expectation_result_gap.v1
version: v1
category: conflict_and_emotion
principle: Let action produce a result that differs meaningfully from the character's expectation, creating new pressure that demands another choice.
rationale: The gap between intended and actual result generates movement without relying on arbitrary interruption.
when_to_use:
  - Designing turning points and escalating scene outcomes.
  - Preventing a protagonist's first tactic from solving the conflict too easily.
when_not_to_use:
  - To make every plan fail identically.
  - When fulfillment itself is the earned emotional payoff.
application_rules:
  - State the expected result before evaluating the actual outcome.
  - Make the unexpected result causally connected to the attempted action.
anti_patterns:
  - Coincidence blocks success without relation to prior choices.
  - Failure changes nothing about the next tactic.
examples:
  - weak: Her evidence upload fails because the internet randomly stops.
  - stronger: Uploading the evidence reveals that the antagonist controls the archive and identifies her source.

knowledge_id: knowledge.platform.tiktok.sound_function.v1
version: v1
category: short_form_tiktok
principle: Assign sound a narrative function such as setting mood, revealing off-screen information, marking change, or creating surprise; do not treat it as undifferentiated decoration.
rationale: TikTok's Creative Codes distinguish functions for music, voiceover, narration, and sound effects in platform creative.
when_to_use:
  - Anticipating voiceover, sonic reveals, or emotional transitions in a TikTok-oriented script.
  - Separating information that belongs to image, dialogue, and audio.
when_not_to_use:
  - To require every beat to contain music or effects.
  - To solve weak visual causality with explanatory narration.
application_rules:
  - State what each important sound contributes that the image does not.
  - Preserve story comprehension when decorative music is removed.
anti_patterns:
  - Music is used to declare an emotion the scene has not earned.
  - Voiceover repeats visible action word for word.
examples:
  - weak: Ominous music plays over an otherwise neutral exchange.
  - stronger: A familiar notification sound exposes that the hidden device is still active.
source_reference:
  source_title: TikTok Creative Codes
  source_author_or_organization: TikTok For Business

  publication_reference: First-party advertising creative guidance; sound section.

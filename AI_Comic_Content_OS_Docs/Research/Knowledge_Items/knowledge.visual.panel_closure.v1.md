knowledge_id: knowledge.visual.panel_closure.v1
version: v1
category: visual_narrative
principle: In sequential visual storytelling, plan what the audience must infer between selected images or beats, and provide enough continuity for that inference to be intentional.
rationale: Comics create movement and meaning not only inside panels but through the audience's closure across omitted intervals.
when_to_use:
  - Translating scene action into a sequence of drawable moments.
  - Deciding which intermediate actions can be omitted without losing causality.
when_not_to_use:
  - To specify final panels or storyboard shots in the current MasterScript.
  - When omission would hide the action that causes the next state.
application_rules:
  - Identify the before-state, after-state, and inference required between them.
  - Preserve critical causal actions; compress only what the audience can reconstruct reliably.
anti_patterns:
  - A character or object changes state with no legible intervening cause.
  - Every micro-action is described, eliminating rhythm and selection.
examples:
  - weak: One beat shows a locked door and the next shows an unrelated rooftop escape.
  - stronger: A stolen access card is foregrounded before the next beat reveals the opened secure exit.
source_reference:

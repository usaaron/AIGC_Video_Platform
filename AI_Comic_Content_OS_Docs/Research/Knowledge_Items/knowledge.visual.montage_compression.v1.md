version: v1
category: visual_narrative
principle: Use a compact visual sequence only when several selected images jointly establish necessary context or intensify one emotional movement more efficiently than full scenes.
rationale: The practitioner source identifies fast montage as a way to compress world information or accumulate emotion; this is a production observation, not a universal format rule.
when_to_use:
  - Compressing repeated evidence, world rules, preparation, or emotional accumulation.
  - When each image contributes to the same narrative function.
when_not_to_use:
  - To skip a consequential choice, confrontation, or causal turn that needs full dramatization.
  - As a default substitute for scene construction.
application_rules:
  - Declare the montage's single purpose.
  - Select only images that advance that purpose and lead into the next dramatic state.
anti_patterns:
  - A montage dumps unrelated lore.
  - The protagonist's decisive action occurs inside compression and loses emotional weight.
examples:
  - weak: Random city images explain a complex social system.
  - stronger: Three access denials show the system closing around the engineer, ending on the one terminal that still recognizes her.

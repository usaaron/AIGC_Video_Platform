knowledge_id: knowledge.serialization.episode_contribution.v1
version: v1
category: story_structure_and_serialization
principle: Each episode in a continuing storyline should contribute a specific escalation, revelation, choice, or consequence to the larger story while completing a legible local movement.
rationale: Episode boundaries become meaningful when the audience can identify both what changed now and why that change matters later.
when_to_use:
  - Creating Episode Plans from an approved longform node.
  - Detecting filler or interchangeable episodes.
when_not_to_use:
  - To require every episode to resolve the series-level dramatic question.
  - To attach an unrelated shock solely to manufacture continuation.
application_rules:
  - State the episode's unique contribution to at least one active storyline.
  - Record the irreversible exit state and the obligation handed to a later episode.
  - Remove or merge an episode if later events remain unchanged without it.
anti_patterns:
  - An episode repeats established conflict without changing knowledge, power, relationship, or risk.
  - The cliffhanger has no causal relationship to the episode's actions.
examples:
  - weak: The heroine is warned again and continues investigating.
  - stronger: Her public accusation protects one source but exposes another, forcing a new alliance in the next episode.

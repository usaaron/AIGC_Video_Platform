knowledge_id: knowledge.story.major_beat_map.v1
version: v1
category: story_structure
principle: Before detailed expansion, identify a compact map of the story's largest action and transformation turns so the causal route from initial state to final state is intelligible.
rationale: A small set of major turns can expose a missing middle or unsupported ending before recursive planning multiplies the weakness across many nodes.
when_to_use:
  - Reviewing a Story Bible or root StoryPlanNode before deeper decomposition.
  - Summarizing the structural backbone for human review.
when_not_to_use:
  - To impose the full Save the Cat beat sheet on every branch or episode.
  - To force fixed percentages, page counts, episode numbers, or equal tree depth.
application_rules:
  - Select only turns that materially change goal, strategy, stakes, relationship, knowledge, or identity.
  - Verify that each major turn is caused or enabled by prior action.
  - Treat the map as a diagnostic overlay; retain the content-driven recursive tree as the planning structure.
anti_patterns:
  - Labels are assigned to events that do not change subsequent choices.
  - Every recursive node repeats the same feature-film beat pattern.
examples:
  - weak: The plan lists a beginning, middle, and ending without showing what redirects the protagonist.
  - stronger: The map shows the accusation that starts the investigation, the irreversible alliance that changes its method, the revelation that changes its target, and the final choice that demonstrates transformation.

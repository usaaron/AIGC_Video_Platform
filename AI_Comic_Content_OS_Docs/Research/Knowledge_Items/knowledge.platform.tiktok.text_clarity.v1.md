version: v1
category: short_form_tiktok
principle: Use on-screen text or captions selectively to clarify essential information while preserving visual and dramatic flow.
rationale: TikTok's platform guidance identifies text overlays and captions as native attention and comprehension tools, but the cited evidence concerns advertising creative.
when_to_use:
  - Proper names, time shifts, messages, inaccessible dialogue, or concise contextual facts.
  - Designing a script that anticipates silent or interrupted viewing.
when_not_to_use:
  - To duplicate every spoken line.
  - To explain emotions already visible through action and performance.
application_rules:
  - Give each text element a distinct comprehension function.
  - Keep text short enough to read without obscuring the focal visual action.
anti_patterns:
  - Dense text competes with a critical visual event.
  - Captions supply plot facts absent from the dramatic action solely to repair confusion.
examples:
  - weak: A paragraph explains that the protagonist is afraid while she stands still.
  - stronger: A short timestamp reveals the message arrived before the alleged sender was alive.

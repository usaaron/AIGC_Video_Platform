knowledge_id: knowledge.story.organic_build.v1
version: v1
category: story_structure
principle: Story elements should grow from the premise and define one another, so the plot feels interconnected rather than mechanically assembled from generic beats.
rationale: An interconnected design reduces episodic filler and makes character, conflict, world, and ending feel specific to the same story idea.
when_to_use:
  - Turning a ContentSpec into a scene sequence.
  - Testing whether genre beats arise from the premise or were pasted onto it.
when_not_to_use:
  - As permission to reject all conventional structures.
  - To demand exhaustive planning before a bounded short-form draft.
application_rules:
  - Trace each major scene to the premise, central desire, or resulting consequence.
  - Remove or redesign beats that could be transferred unchanged to an unrelated story.
anti_patterns:
  - Generic genre events with no relationship to the protagonist's specific desire.
  - Act breaks treated as substitutes for internal causality.
examples:
  - weak: A random attack is inserted because the genre is a thriller.
  - stronger: The attack is caused by evidence the protagonist chose to expose.
source_reference:

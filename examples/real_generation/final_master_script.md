# I Do... Expose You

**Logline**: A bride turns her fake wedding into a public revenge trap after discovering her groom helped orchestrate the betrayal she planned to expose.
**Synopsis**: Mara agreed to marry Adrian Vale to access evidence that his family framed her sister, Elena. Seconds before the vows, Mara discovers Adrian personally funded the key witness. Instead of fleeing, she hijacks the wedding livestream and exposes him before every guest. Adrian counters with an impossible claim: Elena planned the marriage. Then Elena appears on-screen with a warning.
**Hook**: I wasn't marrying him for love. I was marrying him for evidence—until I found his name on the payment that destroyed my sister.
**Target Audience**: US TikTok viewers who binge dark romance, betrayal, public revenge, and twist-heavy serialized drama.
**Target Platform**: TikTok
**Language**: en
**Episode Goal**: Reveal the groom's connection to the betrayal, let Mara publicly seize control, and end with conflicting claims that force viewers into the next episode.

## Characters

### Mara Reed
- Role: Bride and revenge strategist
- Description: A controlled, fearless woman who entered a fake marriage to expose the powerful family that ruined her sister.
- Motivation: Clear Elena's name and make everyone responsible confess publicly.

### Adrian Vale
- Role: Groom and suspected betrayer
- Description: A magnetic heir who agreed to a loveless marriage while hiding his role in Mara's family scandal.
- Motivation: Complete his secret deal with Elena while keeping Mara close.

### Elena Reed
- Role: Mara's missing sister
- Description: The woman at the center of the scandal, presumed gone until she interrupts the wedding remotely.
- Motivation: Warn Mara before Adrian's hidden plan reaches its final stage.

## Scenes

### Scene 1: The Receipt Before the Vows
- Purpose: Open with the fake-marriage contradiction and reveal Adrian's link to Elena's betrayal.
- Setting: Wedding aisle, moments before the ceremony
- Beat Summary: Mara walks toward Adrian while checking the final evidence file on her phone. A payment record identifies Adrian as the man who funded the witness against Elena.
- Emotional Shift: Cold confidence to personal betrayal.
- Emotional Objective: Make viewers question whether Mara will run, submit, or strike back.
- Turning Point: Mara sees Adrian's verified signature on the transaction and decides to continue the ceremony.
- Cliffhanger: No

- Mara advances down the aisle without breaking eye contact.
- She opens the payment record hidden behind her bouquet.
- She removes her engagement ring before reaching Adrian.

#### Dialogue

- **Mara Reed** (Confess the revenge premise): I wasn't marrying him for love.
- **Mara Reed** (Reveal the discovery): I was marrying him for evidence.

### Scene 2: The Bride Hijacks the Wedding
- Purpose: Give Mara visible agency by turning the ceremony and livestream into her revenge trap.
- Setting: Wedding altar beneath a wall-sized livestream screen
- Beat Summary: Asked to say her vows, Mara refuses. She triggers a preloaded presentation showing Adrian's payment record to the guests and thousands watching online.
- Emotional Shift: Betrayal to public control.
- Emotional Objective: Deliver the revenge payoff while preserving doubt about Adrian's true motive.
- Turning Point: Adrian remains calm and claims Mara has exposed only half the truth.
- Cliffhanger: No

#### Character Actions

- Mara takes the officiant's microphone.
- She refuses the scripted vow.
- She taps her ring, activating the livestream evidence package.
- She turns the screen toward Adrian and demands an answer.

#### Dialogue

- **Mara Reed** (Reject the ceremony): No. I don't.
- **Mara Reed** (Take public control): But I do have a wedding gift.

### Scene 3: The Missing Sister Goes Live
- Purpose: Reverse the revenge reveal and end on a direct warning from Elena.
- Setting: Wedding altar as the hacked screen flickers
- Beat Summary: Adrian claims Elena hired him to arrange the marriage and her own disappearance. Before he can explain, Elena takes over the livestream and urgently contradicts him. A public reveal flips the power balance while one decisive answer remains withheld.
- Emotional Shift: Shock to Suspense.
- Emotional Objective: Force viewers to choose whom Mara should trust and demand the next episode.
- Turning Point: Elena appears alive and warns Mara that Adrian's story is a lie.
- Cliffhanger: Yes

#### Character Actions

- Adrian steps closer but does not touch Mara.
- He shows her a message carrying Elena's private code.
- The wedding screen abruptly switches to Elena's live feed.
- Mara raises the microphone and confronts both of them.

#### Dialogue

- **Adrian Vale** (Reveal the deeper arrangement): Elena hired me to marry you.
- **Mara Reed** (Challenge his claim): My sister is gone.
